# Repository Instructions

## Art assets

- For any work under `assets/sprites/`, follow `assets/sprites/AGENTS.md`.
- Treat `ART_PLAN.md` as the visual direction and `ASSETS.md` as the authoritative asset list, dimensions, filenames, and palette constraints.
- Preserve Vietnamese character names, dish names, cultural details, and filename IDs exactly as documented.
- Never replace an approved or active art asset without first creating a recoverable backup.
