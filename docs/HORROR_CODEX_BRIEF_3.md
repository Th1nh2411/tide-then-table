# Horror build: Codex brief 3 (one connected village map)

Same rules as [HORROR_ART_BRIEF.md](HORROR_ART_BRIEF.md) ("How to deliver", "Shared style"). The village is
becoming ONE walkable map: Rin's house (with his room inside) and the pier on the waterfront, a village lane
up to the sidewalk restaurant street. These concepts are for image-to-3D (Hunyuan): 1024×1024, one object,
three-quarter view from slightly above, plain light-grey background, no cast shadow, nothing cropped. Save to
`assets/concepts/horror/<name>.png` with a `<name>.md` sidecar. Textures as stated.
